# App Store "What's New" – 1.1.0

Drafted 2026-08-18 for review. Locale: **en-GB** (the only one the listing has).

⚠️ **This covers everything since build 12**, not just sharing. Real users have
been on build 12 since 2026-07-30, so for them this update carries eighteen
days of work at once: sharing, Danish, keep-screen-on, the import fixes and the
Settings redesign. Writing it as "you can now share a recipe" alone would
undersell it and confuse anyone wondering where Danish came from.

Order is deliberate: the new capability first, then the change most likely to
be noticed (the whole app in another language), then the rest.

---

```
Share a recipe with anyone

Send a recipe to friends or family straight from the recipe's menu. Whoever
opens the link sees the dish, and if they have Prep+Eat it opens right there
with a tap to save it into their own kitchen. They don't need an account just
to look.

The app speaks Danish

If your phone is set to Danish, so is Prep+Eat – your plan, your recipes, the
shopping list and settings. Set to anything else it stays in English. Your own
recipes are never translated; they stay in the language you wrote them in.

The screen stays on while you cook

Open a recipe and your phone stays lit until you leave it, so you can prop it
on the worktop and stop poking it with wet hands. There's a switch in Settings
if you'd rather it behaved as usual.

Better recipe imports

Recipes added from a link bring more across. Ingredients that used to go
missing now arrive, amounts read properly on your shopping list, and a page
with no method says so instead of leaving you an empty card.

Tidier settings

Your kitchen and your app settings are easier to tell apart, and leftovers and
ingredient sections have been polished.
```
