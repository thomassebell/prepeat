These are Claude's sketches, not design. Grey boxes on purpose — they exist to be argued with. Nothing here has been near Figma or the DS, and nothing here is a decision.

What either one forces you to decide

Which name survives?In A the sheet is open on the loser, so context decides. In B neither is obviously the winner — the app has to pick, or ask.
Pick from what?Only this week's list is short and safe. Everything the kitchen has ever seen is more useful and needs search.
How permanent?This teaches the household forever. Nothing in the app currently un-teaches it — no screen lists what it has learned.
Whose decision?One member merges, everyone's list changes. Same as categories today — worth confirming that still feels right at this size.
Also considered and not drawn: drag one row onto another to merge. No new chrome at all, but it collides with the drag already used for reordering categories, and nobody would ever discover it.

Claude's leaning, recorded for whoever picks this up: B for the moment of asking, A as the escape hatch — B catches the common case with no hunting, A stays for the ones the app doesn't spot. That is two surfaces, so it is also the expensive answer.
